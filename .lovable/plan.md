# Three membership levels

- Keep the larger Create free account button above the homepage pictures.
- Allow Basic signup without LinkedIn: profile and browsing only.
- Grant posting, messaging and introductions after verified LinkedIn ownership. A pasted profile URL is not verification.
- Grant full member features and a black ID card through paid membership, without granting admin or founder-private access.
- Preserve current members while transitioning from existing membership rules.

## Safe rollout
- Reconcile existing Executive approval, paid subscriptions and identity cards before activating the new rules.
- Add forward-only permission enforcement for direct writes and member actions; do not rely on hidden buttons.
- Test ownership, payment changes, denied actions and existing-member access in an isolated database before applying live changes.
- Enable LinkedIn ownership verification through the configured provider; it is currently unavailable.
- Verify signup and each level with real accounts before publication. Nothing is published by this plan.

## Current status
The larger signup action is implemented and checked. Membership definitions and presentation are prepared; eight focused tests pass. New access enforcement and paid-card eligibility are not active.
